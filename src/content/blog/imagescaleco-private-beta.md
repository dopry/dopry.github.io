Join the [ImageScale.co](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920005717/http://imagescale.co/#join) Private Beta. Around the beginning of the year I ran in to [Benjamin Melançon](https://drupal.org/user/64383) of [Agaric](http://agaric.com/) . We got to talking about [ImageCache](https://drupal.org/project/imagecache) . In the course of the conversation Ben suggested that I build ImageCache as a service. I told him only if he helped. After a few discussions we decided to make the projecta joint venture between [Agaric](http://agaric.com/) and [Spry Group](http://spry-goup.com/) What we ended up building was [ImageScale](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920005717/http://imagescale.co/) , an image transforming CDN.

Now that we've built our MVP, we would loveyour feedback. If you're a developer and you're interested please sign up at [ImageScale.co](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920005717/http://imagescale.co/#join) . We're going to be inducting new users about every two weeks. If you're a Drupal user, we encourage you to try the [ImageScale](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920005717/https://drupal.org/project/imagescale) module. We're looking for feedback about how the service could help you. We are especially interested in ideas related to responsive images and server side image processing. Keep in mind this is a private beta and isn't production ready.
