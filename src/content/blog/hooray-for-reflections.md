Today, I finally got around to setting up reflection.js. I must say, these little javascript doodads are awesome and
easy to setup. I'm going to slowly turn this garland theme into my own. :)

If you haven't caught on the craze... It's as simple as loading [jquery](https://web.archive.org/web/20120526004753/http://jquery.com/) and [reflection](https://web.archive.org/web/20120526004753/http://cow.neondragon.net/stuff/reflection/) then adding a class='reflect' to your image.
